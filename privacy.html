Privacy Policy — Fight Timer

Last updated 20 August 2026

Fight Timer is a round timer. It does not collect, transmit or sell any personal data. There is no account, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking of any kind.

What the app stores

The app saves your own settings on your device, using the browser's local storage, so it opens the way you left it:

  • number of rounds, round length, rest length
  • your saved “My fight” format
  • cue volume, colour theme and light/dark mode

That data never leaves the device. It is not readable by us or by anyone else, and it is deleted when you uninstall the app or clear its storage.

What the app sends

Nothing. After it is installed, Fight Timer makes no network requests at all — it is designed to run entirely offline, which is also why the timer keeps working in a gym with no signal.

Permissions

  • Keep the screen awake — used only while a fight is running, so the display does not sleep between rounds. Released as soon as the fight ends.
  • Vibration — used for the round and ten-second cues, on devices that support it.

The app asks for no camera, microphone, location, contacts, files or network permission, and no identifiers are read.

Links to other services

The app contains one link out: a Buy me a coffee button that opens ko-fi.com/fighttimer in your browser. Following it is entirely optional. Once you are on Ko-fi you are on their site, under Ko-fi's privacy policy, and any payment is handled by them — Fight Timer never sees payment details and receives no data back.

Children

The app is a timer and is suitable for all ages. Because it collects no data, it collects none from children either.
